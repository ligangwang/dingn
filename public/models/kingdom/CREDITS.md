# Kingdom model credits

The kingdom's buildings, scenery and villagers are low-poly models from
[Polyfork](https://polyfork.dev/), licensed under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Most come from
Polyfork's Medieval Village kit; the sailing boat is scaled up from a toy-sized
model. The files here were optimized (deduplicated,
quantized, and the animated ones trimmed to the one animation we play); the shapes are
unchanged.

The keep, castle and palace walls and towers are drawn in code
(`src/components/kingdom-models.ts`) and are not Polyfork models.

## Landscape

The sky, the ground and the forest trees are made from
[Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/) assets, which are
[CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) (public domain, no
credit required; listed here so the sources stay known). The files are in
`land/`. The sky is the top half of the HDRI, tone-compressed to a JPG; the
ground pictures are resized; the trees are rendered from eight sides into
picture sheets, so no tree mesh is shipped.
